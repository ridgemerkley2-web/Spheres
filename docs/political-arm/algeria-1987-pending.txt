FETCHED FACTS
- 1987 National Assembly: 26 February 1987; FLN sole list, "885 candidates for the
  295 seats, with voters asked to express their preference by crossing out names";
  FLN 295/295, 9,910,631 votes, 100%, turnout 87.48%; "only 67 of the 132
  incumbents who ran for re-election were successful". (1987 election page)
- Term: deputies "are elected for five-year terms" (countrystudies.us/algeria/119),
  so the 1987 chamber's constitutional expiry was February 1992.
- Constitution: amended 3 November 1988 to allow parties other than the FLN
  (Civil War page); new constitution "approved in a referendum by 73% on 23 February
  1989", "introduced a multi-party system, removing the FLN from its role as leading
  party" (Constitution page).
- FIS: founded 18 February 1989 (Abbassi Madani, Ali Belhadj); "officially
  recognised" 16 September 1989. (FIS page)
- Other parties legal by 1 January 1990: RCD "founded 1989", Said Sadi, secularist
  liberal (RCD page); PSD founded 2 March 1989, applied for registration 19 July
  1989 (PSD page); PRA "established in 1989", Boukrouh, liberalism (PRA page); PNSD
  "established in 1989" (PNSD page); PAGS "legalized in 1989", Marxist (MDS page).
  FFS: page says "Legalised 1990" - so NOT present on 1 January 1990 by that source;
  not entered (it is also absent from the existing block).
- What the model must PRODUCE, not be given: local elections 12 June 1990, turnout
  65.2% (8,366,760 of 12,841,769): FIS 4,331,472 (54.2%, 856 communes, 31 wilayas),
  FLN 2,245,798 (28.1%, 486 communes), RCD 2.1%, PNSD 1.6%, PSD 1.1%, PRA 0.8%,
  PAGS 0.3%, independents 11.7% (1990 local page). Legislative first round
  26 December 1991, turnout 59%: FIS 3,260,222 (47.27%, 188 of the 232 seats
  decided), FLN 23.38% (16), FFS 7.40% (25); second round due 16 January 1992,
  cancelled 11 January 1992 (1991 election page, FIS page).
- Next due: the election was set for 26 June 1991 ("The vote was to have been held
  on June 26, 1991", HRW) and postponed to December after the FIS strike of May and
  the state of siege of 5 June 1991 (HRW; Civil War page). NOT SOURCED THIS RUN: the
  date on which the June 1991 date was first announced, so whether it was already
  the scheduled date on 1 January 1990 is not established; the constitutional
  five-year term (expiry February 1992) is the only date that is.

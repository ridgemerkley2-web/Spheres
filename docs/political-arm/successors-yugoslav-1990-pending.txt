R3(a) THE YUGOSLAV SUCCESSORS - 1990 republican elections, TRANSCRIBED 2026-09-06
=================================================================================
Rule: each successor carries the LAST ELECTION BEFORE ITS OWN BIRTH as its dormant
table. For all six that is the 1990 republican election, which is what the existing
POLITIES blocks already hold. Every figure below was fetched THIS RUN from the URL
on the row; where the fetched figure differs from the figure already in POLITIES the
difference is stated and the fetched figure is the one written. Existing party ids
are kept unchanged. Rows marked NEW ROW are additions the integrator may take or
leave; rows in the NOT ENTERED appendix are refused for the reason given.

Syntax is the existing one: p(id, name, native, Family, start[.aligned(Bloc)]),
next: (0, 0) for a successor (dormant until birth), term_months 48 as existing.

Vote shares are the FIRST-ROUND / list vote for the chamber named, as a fraction to
four places, exactly as the fetched page prints them. Seat counts are in comments.

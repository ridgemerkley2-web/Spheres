# South Africa party-office research, 1990–7 September 2026

The companion JSON inventories all seven represented rows and stages **22 people and 18 partial terms**. Complete party and year coverage is still unfinished. Real history stops on 7 September 2026; post-cutoff fictional successors stay separate.

The January 1990 ANC president was **Oliver Tambo**, not Nelson Mandela. Mandela became ANC president in July 1991, and his later state presidency is a separate office. The ANC's [1991 retrospective](https://www.anc1912.org.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/ANC-Today-1-July-2022.pdf) supplies the party handover. **F. W. de Klerk** held NP leadership, **Mangosuthu Buthelezi** headed Inkatha, and **Zephania Mothopeng** held the PAC presidency. These offices must not automatically replace the state executive.

The DP began with **three co-leaders**: Zach de Beer, Denis Worrall and Wynand Malan. The [contemporary SAIRR survey](https://sahistory.org.za/sites/default/files/SAIRR%20Survey%201988-89.pdf) and [following survey](https://www.nelsonmandela.org/omalley/cis/omalley/OMalleyWeb/dat/SAIRR%20Survey%201991-92.pdf) require preserving that collective leadership and the uncertain 1990 transition. A simplified later party timeline is not sufficient to replace them with one person.

There is **no 1990 leader for Freedom Front or ACDP**: they formed in 1994 and December 1993 respectively. [Parliament's constitutional-history volume](https://www.parliament.gov.za/storage/app/media/ProjectsAndEvents/20_years_of_the_constitution/books/Final_Bill_of_Rights_Book_PRINTv3.pdf) identifies Kenneth Meshoe as the ACDP's founding president in December 1993. Those two game rows need pre-formation guards.

Current-history research also matters: the DA's [official 12 April 2026 result](https://www.da.org.za/2026/04/da-announces-leadership-election-results) elected **Geordin Hill-Lewis** federal leader. Leaving John Steenhuisen as party leader through September 2026 would be incorrect. This is distinct from parliamentary and cabinet appointments.

Remaining work includes the NP/NNP office end between its 2005 dissolution decision and legal closure, old DP/DA handovers, exact Freedom Front leadership changes, ACDP near-cutoff confirmation and the PAC's disputed leadership claims. The [2020 PAC judgment](https://www.saflii.org/za/cases/ZAGPPHC/2020/294.html) distinguishes party president and parliamentary representative; neither a historical settlement nor a faction's current website resolves every subsequent claim.

People and avatar tasks must not be counted as completed portraits. The dated Tambo/Sweden reference is inventoried, but its public image download returned HTTP 403 and no local photograph or generated asset is claimed. All saved identity fields, especially de Klerk's existing person ID and birth date, are preserved.

The 2026 DA election is staged as a related-role observation, not an original DP term: DP/DA alliance and legal integration boundaries still need a separate identity migration. Brazil’s TSE-certified party renamings are similarly name eras, not invented coalition components.

The [IEC’s August2006 statement](https://www.gov.za/news/independent-electoral-commission-cancellation-registration-political-parties-31-aug-2006) confirms final NNP registration cancellation effective **3July2006**. The JSON now records that legal dissolution bound while preserving the separate leadership-end gap.

# China leadership research — 7 September 2026

The simulation currently represents only `cn_cpc`. Three formal General Secretary terms are staged: Jiang Zemin from 24 June 1989, Hu Jintao from 15 November 2002, and Xi Jinping from 15 November 2012. The archive and contemporary leadership statements support those transitions. [CPC 1989 archive](https://cpc.people.com.cn/BIG5/64162/64165/66002/4463025.html), [Hu's 2002 announcement](https://news.cctv.com/news/china/20021115/100209.shtml), [Xi's 2012 announcement](https://us.china-embassy.gov.cn/eng/zt/18th_CPC_National_Congress_Eng/201211/t20121115_4917562.htm).

General Secretary, PRC President, Premier, Party CMC chairman, state CMC chairman and informal paramount leadership are distinct roles. Existing Jiang identity fields and office links must survive ingestion. In particular, do not replace the formal party leader with Deng Xiaoping merely because a campaign-start source discusses Deng's influence. Collective Politburo Standing Committee membership needs a separate member-by-member timeline.

The current-office interpretation uses a direct June 2026 General Secretary reference and the official September 7 party-core reference. It does not predict Xi's future tenure. Hu and Xi birth dates remain at month precision because the primary biographies used here do not supply exact days. [June 2026 official report](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202606/08/content_WS6a269d70c6d00ca5f9a0b75f.html), [September 7 report](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202609/07/content_WS6a9e7862c6d00ca5f9a0d0ab.html).

The eight recognized non-CPC parties are explicitly inventoried as missing game identities, **not CPC coalition components**. Their chair histories and avatars still require work. Hong Kong and Macao organizations also need a territorial/representation audit. [Official multiparty-system white paper](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/topnews/202106/26/content_WS60d66223c6d0df57f98dbe58.html).

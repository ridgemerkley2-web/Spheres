## Source and date decisions

- [Hanns Seidel Foundation archive chair list](https://www.hss.de/fileadmin/user_upload/HSS/Dokumente/ACSP/CSU/CSU-Parteivorsitzende.pdf), page 1, supplies exact CSU handovers. Theo Waigel begins 19 November 1988; do not substitute 16 November. The incoming chair owns the handover day.
- [KAS CDU board archive](https://www.kas.de/en/web/geschichte-der-cdu/bundesvorstandsmitglieder-seit-1998) separates party and parliamentary chairs. Schäuble's February 2000 resignation announcement versus formal tenure end remains an explicit gap. January 2021 and 2022 digital elections/postal confirmation retain month precision.
- [SPD 2019/2020 yearbook](https://spd05.spd.de/fileadmin/pv/Dokumente/BPT2021_Dez/Berichte/SPD_PV_Jahrbuch_2019_2020.pdf) ends Thorsten Schäfer-Gümbel's acting service on 30 September 2019; exclusive end is 1 October. It overrides the contradictory 6 December entry in the Bundestag handbook. Dreyer, Schwesig and Schäfer-Gümbel are separate simultaneous acting officeholders.
- [FDP's 30 May 2026 election results](https://www.fdp.de/pressemitteilung/fdp-bundesparteitag-wahlergebnisse-praesidium-4) name Wolfgang Kubicki as current chair after Christian Dürr. The file does not extrapolate 2025 leadership.
- [Heinrich Böll Foundation congress inventory](https://www.boell.de/sites/default/files/uploads/2008/08/bdk_1979-1993_die_gruenen.pdf) establishes the 1989 Fücks/Hammerbacher/Krieger trio and the 1990 Damus/Rühle/Ströbele trio. Multi-day congress dates remain month precision unless an actual election day is evidenced.

All intervals are half-open. Coarse month/year boundaries can conservatively create unresolved display gaps; these do not claim historical vacancies. Unverified acting subphases, such as earlier SPD changes, are not invented. Some starter coverage is clipped at 1 January 1990 and is labeled accordingly. Missing birth/death fields remain unverified.

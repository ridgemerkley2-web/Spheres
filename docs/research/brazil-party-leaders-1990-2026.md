# Brazil party-office research, 1990–7 September 2026

The companion JSON inventories all six represented rows and stages **29 people and 28 partial terms**. It does not claim all Brazilian parties, complete historical coverage, or finished avatars. Source-era historical records stop on 7 September 2026; fictional successors after that date belong to the separate future catalog.

January 1990 has three straightforward organizational leaders: **Hugo Napoleão (PFL), Luiz Gushiken (PT), and Leonel Brizola (PDT)**. PMDB needs both the titular Ulysses Guimarães and acting Jarbas Vasconcelos roles displayed correctly. The [party's own table](https://www.mdb.org.br/quem-somos/) dates Jarbas's acting period to 26 April 1989–2 January 1990, and the [December 1989 Chamber record](https://imagem.camara.leg.br/Imagem/d/pdf/DCD08DEZ1989.pdf) expressly identifies acting status.

PRN and PDS remain January gaps. [Contemporary February 1990 reporting](https://hemeroteca-pdf.bn.gov.br/325538/per325538_1990_00212.pdf) identifies Daniel Tourinho as national PRN president, but it does not prove an exact January accession. Fernando Collor was the presidential candidate, which is a different role. [The March 1992 Chamber record](https://imagem.camara.gov.br/Imagem/d/pdf/DCD19MAR1992.pdf) identifies Paulo Maluf as PDS president; it cannot establish that he already held the same office in January 1990. Jarbas Passarinho's late-1989 leave must be resolved first.

The [TSE registry and transformation tables](https://www.tse.jus.br/partidos/partidos-registrados-no-tse) distinguish PFL becoming DEM, DEM merging into União Brasil in February 2022, and PRN/PTC/AGIR name changes. [FGV's organizational history](https://atlas.fgv.br/verbete/6099) dates the PDS/PDC merger into PPR to April 1993. These are lifecycle transitions, not evidence that every 1990 party continues unchanged in the present.

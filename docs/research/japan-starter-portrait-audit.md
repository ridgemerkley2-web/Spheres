# Japan starter portrait reference audit

Checked September 7, 2026. These are observed appearance references for original cartoon artwork. No source photograph is added to game assets, and no free-use license is asserted.

## Eiichi Nagasue (`eiichi_nagasue`, 永末英一)

Source: [1989 All Japan Junior Badminton Championships program](https://www.badminton-archive.com/download/tournament/1989/26/12/1/1), PDF page 4 / printed page 2, right-hand portrait. The caption names Nagasue as Kyoto Prefecture Badminton Association president. His greeting is dated November 10, 1989; PDF page 3 / printed page 1 gives the event as November 10-12, 1989. The photograph's capture date is not specified, so 1989 is its verified publication context, not an asserted capture date.

Observed appearance: very thick, oversized dark rectangular glasses with rounded corners; broad smiling face with full lower cheeks and rounded chin; dense dark hair swept up and back into a rounded silhouette above a high clear forehead; clean-shaven; dark suit and white shirt. Monochrome printing does not establish precise skin, hair, eye, suit, or tie colors. The bald man on the left is Yoshio Sakurauchi, not Nagasue.

Rights: the program and photograph remain source material; ownership/license was not established. Use the appearance observations for original cartoons, without reproducing the page or photograph in the game.

## Tetsuzo Fuwa (`tetsuzo_fuwa`, 不破哲三)

Source: [JNPC bulletin, May 10, 1989, number 231](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/jnpc-prd-public-oregon/files/1989/05/jnpc-b-198905.pdf), PDF/printed page 12, small lower-right portrait explicitly captioned with Fuwa's name and then-role as JCP vice-chair. The article continues onto page 13 and identifies the event as April 18. The [JNPC event record](https://www.jnpc.or.jp/archive/conferences/20054/report) independently confirms April 18, 1989. The photo is published as part of that contemporary event report; no separate capture-date metadata was found.

Observed appearance: neatly side-parted short dark hair swept across the top with modest volume; oval-to-rounded face, full cheeks and rounded chin; large dark rounded-rectangular eyeglasses, less oversized and thick than Nagasue's; clean-shaven; mild open-mouth speaking smile; medium-tone suit, white shirt and dark tie. Monochrome printing does not establish exact colors. Do not copy his much later white-haired appearance for the 1990 starting avatar. The large photograph above depicts a different event and is not Fuwa's portrait.

Rights: JNPC's [event site](https://www.jnpc.or.jp/archive/conferences/20132/report) explicitly states all rights reserved. Reference only; no free-license claim or source-photo redistribution.

## Exact 1990 group reference

The [JNPC March 10, 1990 bulletin, number 241](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/jnpc-prd-public-oregon/files/1990/03/jnpc-b-199003.pdf), PDF/printed pages 10-11, was downloaded and visually inspected. It depicts the February 2, 1990 five-party debate, consistent with the [organizer's event record](https://www.jnpc.or.jp/archive/conferences/20132/report). The group faces are too small to derive dependable facial details. It confirms the shared event context but is not the main likeness reference.

Temporary local PDFs and full-page renders were kept outside the repository for the art agent. No cropped or modified source photograph was created. The research registry remains unchanged by this visual audit.

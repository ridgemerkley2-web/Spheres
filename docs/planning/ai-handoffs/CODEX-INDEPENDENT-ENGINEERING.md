# Independent campaign engineering

Owner: Codex. Authorized order: recovery, long-campaign stability, release
packaging. These bounded engineering tasks do not depend on additional Claude
research. They do not replace S23, the complete S24/S25 qualification matrices,
independent S26 human sessions, or the S27/S28 frozen release requirements.

## CODEX-S24-RECOVERY-01

Repair reproduced save/recovery defects. Keep missing-primary backups visible,
preserve the previous recovery point after a repeated save, and clean up only
temporary files the operation actually created. Exercise a real lost save reply
and explicit backup loading at desktop and narrow widths. At active construction,
delivery, refit, rebasing and mission boundaries, lose an actually committed day
reply, reload/retry it, compare complete campaign archives and reject the old
receipt after a new load session. Retain fixture provenance and failures.

Completion requires native regression checks, actual browser results and a
reviewable evidence packet. Worldwide startup qualification remains separate.

## CODEX-S25-RUNNER-01

Build a reproducible native runner with ordinary startup/commands, explicit
invariants, scheduled native save/resume comparisons and failure records. Freeze
the requested countries/seeds/dates before running; reject missing, duplicate,
short or mismatched results. Exercise a paired pilot across month/year boundaries.
Track the complete eight-country, three-seed matrix separately from pilot success.
Settling 31 December 2035 reaches native date 1 January 2036 and its normal pause.

Completion of the runner and pilot cannot complete the full S25 session. The
controlled USSR-to-Russia continuation and later full-matrix evidence remain
explicitly accounted for.

## CODEX-S28-PACKAGE-01

Verify the packaged executable's embedded identity before labeling a release.
Refuse existing archive/patch destinations, make archive metadata deterministic,
include locally patched dependency licenses, and exercise extraction, offline
first run and save/recovery in a disposable directory. Add equivalent Windows
and Linux CI packaging checks and preserve results for the exact candidate.

Tooling/local smoke completion does not award S28: its final frozen candidate,
both remote platform results and clean-machine evidence remain required.

## Source and integrated behavior

The original active ownership baseline is `5f7f355502f17bd6bd8f0383a2d14f0024fa7884`; selected operational warfare and coupled map code comes from pinned master `485c223f60d5ff6e46f6ae17164bf1ee3a8764d9`. These source identities have different roles; none is interchangeable with a staging patch base.

Operational orders allocate the nation’s existing forces to reserves, sectors and dated journeys. Preparation, local engagements and final national settlement share one opening `operations::Snapshot`. Local combat must not charge a second force, equipment, ammunition or population loss. Unsupported capabilities remain unavailable. This is an arcade strategy layer; detailed aircraft inspection is separate from campaign mission support.

`campaign_supply` moves finite support services from sources into booked cargo and local buffers, sharing actual freight capacity with commerce. Services are not physical ammunition, oil or equipment. Physical stores remain owned and consumed by their existing systems. A blocked trip retains its dated property instead of recreating stock or granting immediate support.

Occupation changes control and site operation without awarding the occupier a company’s stock, money, intellectual property or contracts. Limited peace requires current coalition consent. Cession invokes the existing district/resident/GDP transfer, while reparations use the existing cash-transfer owner. A political opening changes institutional conditions and invokes existing election/eligibility hooks; it does not infer a national leader from a party-chair title.

The integrated save envelope declares warfare alongside retained equipment, party, economy and company capabilities. Legacy loading does not adopt warfare, issue orders, move troops or settle peace. Validation preserves recognized dated historical property, rejects incompatible versions and unknown nested property, and retains identity high-water marks. Fresh startup explicitly adopting the unified capabilities is S05 behavior, separate from ordinary load.

Map integration keeps camera, relief, city placement, labels and picking together. The high zoom ceiling does not increase the approximately **1,855 m** spacing of the elevation source. Streets/buildings remain procedural and representative. The final candidate also corrects the inherited mismatch between city placement and rendered water described below.

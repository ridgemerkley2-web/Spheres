# S08 — Supplier choice and reviewed imports

Status: in progress. Authorized by “Next” after S07; stop before S09.

Implement finite domestic and foreign supplier offers, explicit import adoption, reviewed purchases and cancellations, conserved escrow/delivery, frozen foreign specifications without component-research grants, and a paid path to stock for governments without a domestic arms industry.

Seven bounded, modeled supplier programmes use ordinary national construction budgets, company capital, paid development, tooling and actual inputs. They are not historical firms or free starting fleets. Autonomous orders skip the player and pause without economic competition; legacy loads remain opt-in. The UI shows dated work and blockers rather than inventing a first-stock date. Compatible ammunition commissioning first adopts ordinary upkeep for the supplier nation's actual existing fleet; subsequent invoices remain limited by its maintenance appropriation.

Open **Government → Companies → Compare equipment suppliers**. Activate a departmental budget, then review import adoption. The Exchange offers the existing economic-competition decision that allows autonomous suppliers to work. Filter domestic/foreign offers and ready/affordable stock, inspect the exact model, select a quantity and confirm the current native price. Paid deliveries remain visible alongside domestic orders. An undelivered import can be returned through a separately reviewed cancellation when the original seller has space to receive its stock.

Imports reserve actual finished units; they do not commission a government production line. The original Defense payment settles once into import escrow, and the seller earns it on delivery. A cancellation returns the original cost basis and refunds the settled buyer payment once without recreating spending authority. Route and shipping-province access are checked throughout transit. Imported models retain their exact specifications and ordinary use/maintenance rights without granting component research or a domestic manufacturing licence.

Money commitments show the original unsettled transfer and then the buyer's held import escrow with its owned lot and current shipping reason. Imports are separate from domestic paid deliveries; no second Treasury bill or combined commitment total is invented.

The genuine Tonga qualification uses an explicit budget choice: transfer 2.5 percentage points of GDP from Infrastructure to Defense while keeping total spending unchanged, and allocate Defense shares of 6% personnel/training, 6% operations, 7% maintenance/supply, 80% procurement and 1% military research. The opening review prices its political cost and provides $3.0096 million of annual procurement at opening GDP; it grants no immediate cash or authority. It reduces infrastructure appropriations; the chosen personnel, operations and maintenance allocations exceed their positive opening amounts. Annual renewal retains this chosen policy. The earlier unchanged-default-budget run is retained separately: actual French APC stock cost about $1.84 million per unit, above Tonga's default appropriation.

Development and tooling consume paid utilities before an inventory unit exists. These costs now have an explicit cumulative preproduction classification in the supplier account, counted once by the save validator. Older missing fields are recovered only when the saved phase and inputs uniquely prove all costs preceded any physical unit; explicit corrupt values and ambiguous later production histories are refused. Valid old default/grandfathered accounts retain their original representation.

Qualification will include the existing eleven-platform domestic lifecycle, new foreign-ownership and representative ammunition tests, fresh-world earned supplier stock and a Tonga import browser journey, save preservation, both native/UI platforms and measured performance on the pinned implementation. Completion and exact evidence remain pending.

This is a bounded S08 market qualification, not a historical opening fleet or a full 1990–2035 campaign certificate. Fresh modeled suppliers must construct facilities and earn their first stock; the development run reached French APC stock in 1995. Historical firms/opening catalogues remain E05. The complete military/ammunition matrix uses explicitly synthetic capacity/funding fixtures; those tests do not establish fresh-world availability of every platform or ammunition family.

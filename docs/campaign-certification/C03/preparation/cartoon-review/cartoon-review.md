Each 1024x1536 master PNG was viewed at full resolution. Card-size renderings at the game's government card (106x152), narrow card (90x132) and featured leadership card (156x218) sizes were made with Pillow LANCZOS, object-fit contain and bottom alignment in a scratch folder that is not committed. Faces and hands were cropped and viewed at native resolution. Figure bounding boxes, margins, background colours and fabric-to-background luminance contrast were measured from pixels. Likeness was compared only with the reference photographs retained in the repository (Tupou IV, Kaifu and Doi); the other references are not in the repository and were not downloaded, so those entries compare the drawing with its recorded prompt instead.

Across the sample:

- At 106x152 every sampled face is about 18 px wide and about 25 px tall, so identity on small cards rests on hair, costume colour and silhouette rather than facial likeness. The person-portrait schema already accepts an optional normalized crop; a reviewed head-and-shoulders card crop would need a separate UI change.
- All sampled backgrounds are the approved opaque dark teal (#0f222a to #152830 against the anchor's #152830), so background consistency holds.
- Face rendering drifts from the anchor: several faces carry dense painterly shading and fine wrinkles where the anchor uses flat cel shading and a bold contour.
- Figure scale on cards varies with margins and pose: top margins range from 33 px (Brizola, raised arms) to 70 px (anchor), so fitted figures appear at different sizes side by side.
- Dark garments separate from the teal background mainly through the outline (luminance contrast 1.0-1.2:1 even for the anchor's navy skirt); garments that also match the background hue, such as Nakano Emi's teal suit, nearly vanish at card size.

## Limitations

- Dimensions come from file headers; pixel data is not decoded, so corruption after a valid header is not detected here.
- Duplicates are exact SHA-256 matches; re-encoded, resized or cropped near-duplicates are not detected.
- Identity references that are not shipped (most historical references) cannot be compared by this tool; their rights are reported as gaps, not judged.
- The file inventory is the git index when available; untracked files are ignored.
- Countries and art windows come from the checked-in production inventory, whose recorded input hashes are compared with the current manifests and registry.
- Automated findings never approve artwork; the visual review sample records proposals only.

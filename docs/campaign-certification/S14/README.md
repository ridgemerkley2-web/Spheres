# S14 — Fund routine aircraft support

Status: implemented; qualification in progress with S12–S15.

One reviewed **Fleet upkeep + aircraft stores** cap applies prospectively. On each authorized day it pays the existing whole-fleet maintenance invoice first, then buys compatible finished aircraft stores from allowed company offers with any remaining cap and Defense authority. An existing lower normal maintenance preference does not prevent the active combined policy from funding upkeep; that normal preference resumes when the combined policy is disabled.

The policy buys no aircraft, creates no supplier, enrolls no import permission and grants no additional fiscal authority. It counts delivered stores, paid inbound deliveries and unfinished public ammunition work before ordering the remaining gap. Separately ordered work retains its own explicit instructions. Supplier payments and deliveries stay in their existing ledgers.

Fully funded upkeep advances eligible post-flight service by at most one day. Transit, inaccessible bases and insufficient maintenance prevent recovery. The screen explains family shortages, receipts and pending policy changes. Edits and toggles cannot reopen a settled day or repeat a purchase.

The [combined flight qualification](../S15/README.md) covers zero/partial funding, financial caps, compatible stores, exact delivery receipts, prospective changes, save/resume and service recovery. This session does not complete G3.

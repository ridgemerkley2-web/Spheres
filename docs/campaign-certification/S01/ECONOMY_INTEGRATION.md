The accepted product contract is financial construction paid from an assigned construction budget as work progresses. Equipment is designed, developed and built by a company, enters company stock, and is bought by the country. Integration must preserve that lifecycle and the active save entitlements.

The principal conflict is in executable code: master retains the zero-material `project_recipe` helper but bypasses it in the rebuilt path, which consumes catalogue materials and requires assigned construction capacity. Loading a save enables this path automatically. Comments describing financial-only construction therefore cannot be used as evidence of the master runtime behavior.

Base prices are defined together at `M:industry.rs:235–252`. The commodity vector order is `[Bauxite, Coal, Cobalt, Copper, Gas, Gold, Iron, Oil, Phosphate, PlatinumGroup, RareEarths, Uranium]`, defined by `resources::Commodity` and `resources::ALL`.

The label changes are not all cosmetic. `civilian_industry` changes from an enabling industrial estate into up to 10 units of staffed, powered construction capacity per level (`M:production.rs:308–312`). Infrastructure gains a 10% local construction work bonus per level (`M:production.rs:300`). Those behavior changes require an explicit integration decision; keeping a key does not preserve what the player bought.

Master construction may substitute intermediate packs for iron, and one capital-goods pack for 0.1 copper, before reserving remaining raw materials (`M:industry.rs:458–486`). Its recurring goods and raw-demand calculations also incorporate this construction demand (`M:commerce.rs:308–319`; `M:industry.rs:788–803,837–843`). Removing only the settlement draw would leave misleading demand, import, suggestion and preview behavior.

## Suggested S02 integration sequence

These steps are proposed integration work after S01. They are not authorization to alter the runtime during the baseline freeze.

1. **Set schema and entitlement ownership.** Retain A company-stock and historical-party books, envelopes and validators. Append the new project/good identities and inert fields without enabling new rules. Define explicit imported-M-save treatment and preserve counters, ledgers, reservations and dates.
2. **Reconcile financial construction end to end.** Integrate useful catalogue/UI additions while retaining the accepted funding allocator, frozen contracts and fractional commissioning. Resolve ECON-01, ECON-02 and ECON-04 together, including construction/mine previews, demand and contractor service fees.
3. **Reconcile equipment facilities and company roles.** Keep development → certification → company fabrication → company stock → purchase → delivery. Add service-company identity/assignments as a distinct role or explicit mapping; preserve leases and paid lines. Decide grandfathering before activating Shipyard or advanced-component prerequisites.
4. **Integrate population behind explicit enrollment.** Preserve current residents, share operation job recipes, wire authored succession transfers, and publish one labor/unemployment result before economy. Validate coherent saved outcomes and delayed training.
5. **Integrate operating output, GDP and fiscal observation with a documented daily order.** Retain existing processor ownership, add new actual operating receipts and component stock, preserve manufacturer receivable hooks, and observe cash only after the relevant owners settle it. Preserve the identical province GDP reconciliation layer.
6. **Enable the integrated defaults only after migration fixtures pass.** Separate fresh-campaign enrollment from loaded-campaign rules. Establish explicit upgrade behavior and retained envelope support. Attach evidence from the fixtures below before any broader campaign certification claim.

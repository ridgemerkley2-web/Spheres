# S24 successor fixtures — inventory and raw evidence

**Preparation for S24 only (CLAUDE-S24-SUCCESSORS-01).** Every activation recorded here is a labelled
**fixture**: an authored or staged parent collapse on 2 January 1990. It does not prove organic succession,
historical timing or the S25 long-campaign route, and it is not S24 qualification. Full S24 still needs its
canonical dependencies and Codex's exact-build startup and recovery qualification.

## Findings

* **D-activation / D-map (inventory).** 23 identities are expected by the pathway, the census and C01; only 21 can
  be activated. Namibia and East Timor have no dissolution, no `SUCCESSOR_PARENTS` entry, no continuation family and
  no 1990 owner of their `NA-*`/`TL-*` districts. Recorded `unrun`; see proposal P1. The expectation stays 23.
* **O1–O3 (inventory observations).** `sources_json` says all 23 are seated "where the sim seats them" (only 21 are);
  census `existing_lifecycle_records` is also 23 but counts party lifecycle disclosures, not successors; Namibia and
  East Timor are successors by date, not federation breakups.
* **F1 (presentation, informational).** On every activated successor the budget card reads the real-rate floor as
  a "sovereign spread" (Serbia: *real −34.90% +32.90pp sovereign spread at 28% of GDP*, `serbia-after_activation-budget.jpg`).
  The effective rate (−2%) matches the simulation; only the served breakdown and its label disagree. Proposal P3.
  No money semantics are asserted by the harness.

## Reproduce and clean up

```sh
export CARGO_TARGET_DIR=<own target dir>
cargo build --locked --release -p spheres-web && cp $CARGO_TARGET_DIR/release/spheres-web.exe <frozen>.exe
SPHERES_S21_FIXTURE_DIR=<new abs dir> cargo test --locked --release -p spheres-web \
  campaign_journey::tests::s21_export_review_fixtures -- --ignored --exact --nocapture
NODE_PATH=<tools/ui/node_modules with playwright 1.58.2> SPHERES_BROWSER_CHANNEL=chrome \
  node tools/ui/successor-fixtures/run.cjs --binary <frozen>.exe --expected-revision <40-hex HEAD> \
  --out <new abs dir outside the repo> --native-s21 <exporter dir> --native-provenance <record.json> --cross-check
node --test tools/ui/check_successor_fixtures.cjs
node tools/ui/successor-fixtures/inventory.cjs --check docs/campaign-certification/S24/preparation/successor-fixtures/inventory.json
```

The harness stops its server and deletes every disposable archive after hashing it (61 in the committed run,
`cleanup.remaining_saves: []`). Afterwards delete the `--out` directory, the exporter directories and the cargo
target directory. No user campaign or save location is read or written.

## Limits

Fixtures, not history: the parent is collapsed on 2 January 1990 by authored staging. Seven days of continuation
is not a long-run check. Only four identities are browser-driven; the other 17 are covered through the served API.
Map ownership is read from served district ownership, not the WebGL globe. Guidance checks bind advice to the
successor but do not judge its quality. Namibia and East Timor remain unrun until P1 is ruled on.

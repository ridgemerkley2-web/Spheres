### How a holder is dated

The packet follows CLAUDE-C01-18, CLAUDE-C01-29 and CLAUDE-C01-31, and Codex's review 988d5379 of CLAUDE-C01-31. A holder has
`from` only where a source states the day office was assumed and `until` only where a source states the day it ended; otherwise it
has `attested_on`. **No holder in this packet has a start or an end.**

- **What dates an observation.** A party record or the party organ naming the person in the office on a day it prints: a byline
  (日本共産党中央委員会幹部会委員長 不破哲三, 1994年7月19日報告), the congress diary (宮本議長欠席, 23 September 1997), the Secretariat's
  communiqué on a first plenum (不破委員長が発言, 不破哲三議長が…呼びかけた), the introduction to an address or summation on a congress's
  final day (最終日の十四日、志位和夫委員長がおこなった閉会あいさつ), a holder's own statement of the office (日本共産党中央委員会議長の
  不破哲三でございます) and the 国民民主党's same-day records naming 玉木雄一郎代表. Where a page prints no day for the address it reports,
  the observation is dated by the issue date (不破哲三, 12 January 2006, as CLAUDE-C01-29 and CLAUDE-C01-31 did).
- **What is not a start.** 不破哲三's '新しい体制のなかで、私は中央委員会議長の任にあたることになりました' (24 November 2000) is read, like
  竹谷とし子's acceptance in Codex's review 988d5379, as a same-day attestation; the earlier 国民民主党's press conference heading
  '臨時党大会で選出され代表に就任' (4 September 2018) belongs to another organization (claims only). Statements of being chosen or
  reappointed (選出されました, 再任されました) date an observation but are not starts.
- **What never feeds a holder.** First-plenum elections (with or without names), 'new' stylings on an election day (志位和夫新委員長,
  玉木新代表; CLAUDE-C01-27 and CLAUDE-C01-40), convention selections, the 1997 rule change (procedure only), the honorary office
  名誉議長, 不破哲三's withdrawal of January 2006, retrospective records, every record of the 国民民主党 of 2018-2020, the 2024 suspension
  and 古川元久's acting service.
- **No end.** No source reviewed states the day any chair's or representative's office ended: 宮本顕治's approval as 名誉議長 and the
  first plenum's not filling the chair (26 September 1997), the first plenum's election of 志位和夫 as Executive Committee chair (24
  November 2000), the new Central Committee's confirmation of 不破哲三's withdrawal (14 January 2006) and the suspension from posts (4
  December 2024) are claims; no end is inferred from a successor's election.

Names follow the sources in Japanese, one holder string per person (宮本顕治, 不破哲三, 志位和夫, 玉木雄一郎). Where a source prints a
surname and title (宮本議長, 不破委員長, 不破議長, 志位委員長, 玉木代表) the claim text keeps that form. A row's `holder_name` is null where
the row names no holder of its own office: an election that names nobody, procedure, an honorary office, the earlier party's offices,
organization events, the suspension and the acting service. Romanized forms: 宮本顕治 Miyamoto Kenji, 不破哲三 Fuwa Tetsuzō, 志位和夫
Shii Kazuo, 玉木雄一郎 Tamaki Yūichirō; named in claims only: 大塚耕平 Ōtsuka Kōhei and 古川元久 Furukawa Motohisa.

## Observations

### JCP-01 — 1990-1996: the chairs when the period opens

Evidence: the party's copy of the report on the partial revision of the programme to the 20th Congress is bylined
'日本共産党中央委員会幹部会委員長 不破哲三', dated '1994年7月19日報告', and introduced as given on the 19th, the congress's first day; in it
不破哲三 refers to '宮本議長' in the present style.

Decision: accepted in part. Both chairs are observed on 19 July 1994, the first party record reviewed after 1 January 1990.

Limits: no contemporaneous party record of the 19th Congress (July 1990) or of the 1994 elections was found. The Diet minutes name
不破委員長 and 宮本議長 from 1993 in remarks by members whom the records do not identify as party officers, and 志位和夫's statements as
head of the Secretariat (from 1994) name 不破委員長 without the record identifying him as an officer in the same meeting (leads).

### JCP-02 — September 1997: the 21st Congress, the optional chair and 宮本顕治's honorary office

Evidence: the party's diary of the 21st Congress (22-26 September 1997) records the report of 宮本議長's absence on the 23rd, the
adoption of the partial amendment of the rules on the 26th, the approval of the honorary chair and the announcement of the new three
principal officers, and 不破委員長's closing address; the proposal reported on the 25th amends rule 31 so that the Central Committee
'may' elect a 中央委員会議長 ('中央委員会議長をおかないこともありうる'); the honorary officers' page lists '名誉議長 宮本顕治（８８）' among the
new appointments approved on the 26th; the Secretariat's communiqué of the 26th reports that the first plenum elected the Executive
Committee chair, the head of the Secretariat and vice-chairs, and that 不破委員長 spoke.

Decision: accepted in part. 宮本顕治 is observed as 議長 on 23 September 1997 and 不破哲三 as 委員長 on 26 September 1997. The rule
change is procedure only, the honorary office is another office, and the plenum's election names nobody.

Limits: no source states the day 宮本顕治's chairmanship ended; that the plenum filled no Central Committee chair is recorded as a
claim, never as an until.

### JCP-03 — November 2000: the 22nd Congress

Evidence: the opening address of 20 November 2000 is introduced as delivered by 不破哲三委員長; the party's list dated 24 November gives
the four principal officers elected by the first plenum, headed '中央委員会議長 不破哲三' and '幹部会委員長 志位和夫'; in his closing
address on the final day, 24 November, 不破議長 says '新しい体制のなかで、私は中央委員会議長の任にあたることになりました' and that

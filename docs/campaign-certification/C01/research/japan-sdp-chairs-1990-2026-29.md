### How a holder is dated

The packet follows the rule of CLAUDE-C01-12, CLAUDE-C01-13 and CLAUDE-C01-18. A holder has `from` only where a source states
the day office was assumed, and `until` only where a source states the day it ended; otherwise it has `attested_on`. One holder
observation is kept per election or selection found (the LDP and ANC convention), so 土井たか子's 1998 and 2000 elections and
福島瑞穂's 2009, 2012, 2022 and 2023 re-elections have their own observations.

- **What dates an observation.** A same-day party record naming the person as chair or party leader after the selection (a
  meeting report, a signed statement, the heading of an address, a caption, a press-conference report, a party organ's issue),
  or, in the Diet minutes, a party officer's own statement: the chair's own statement of the office, or another officer's
  statement naming the chair where the record identifies him as a party officer (山口鶴男 as 書記長 in 1990, 山花貞夫 as 書記長 in
  1991). Each holder cites only such claims, each carrying exactly the holder's date.
- **What states a start or an end.** Only 福島瑞穂's own statement of the day she became leader (十一月十五日…新党首になりました)
  states a start, and only her own words at the press conference of 25 July 2013 (本日で辞任する), as the party reported them, state
  an end. 就任 wording without a day (新しく…委員長に就任いたしました; 党首就任あいさつ; 新たに新党首に就任させていただきました) dates an
  observation but not the day office took effect. 吉田忠智's recollection of 2018 that he took over on 1 November 2013 states a
  day but conflicts with his own statement of 24 October 2013 that he had already become leader, so it is never a start.
- **What never feeds a holder.** Party elections and selections, notices, filings, counts and declarations, convention
  confirmations (信任), announced, submitted, accepted and withdrawn resignations, the acting leader (党首代行), predecessor
  references (前党首), continuation attestations (a later attestation of the same observation, or the incumbent before a vote),
  schedules and plans, the organization claims and every retrospective record. Party rules are procedure only, never a date.
- **Re-elections.** The party's procedure starts a new two-year term at the next regular convention (the 2013 and 2022 notices
  say so), so an observation dated on or just after a re-election may fall in the previous term; no start is inferred from it.
- **Dates completed from context.** Where a party page prints only a day or a month and day, the year or month comes from the
  page's own issue date, posting date, folder or index (as CLAUDE-C01-18 took years from its election index); each such claim
  says so. A recollection that prints no year (the 1990 party leaders' meeting) gets no structured date.

Names follow the sources in Japanese, one holder string per person (土井たか子, 田邊誠, 山花貞夫, 村山富市, 福島瑞穂, 吉田忠智,
又市征治). The Diet minutes print 田邊誠 and, in 2003, 福島瑞穂; the party's pages print 福島みずほ; every row carries 福島瑞穂 and
the printed form stays in the claim text. Where a source prints only a surname and title (土井委員長, 田邊委員長, 福島党首), the text
keeps that form. A row's `holder_name` is null where the source names no holder of this office: a notice, a schedule, filings by
several candidates, an organization event, or the acting leader. Romanized forms: 土井たか子 Doi Takako, 田邊誠 Tanabe Makoto, 山花貞夫
Yamahana Sadao, 村山富市 Murayama Tomiichi, 福島瑞穂 Fukushima Mizuho, 吉田忠智 Yoshida Tadatomo and 又市征治 Mataichi Seiji.

### Party office, state office and the organization

`jp_sdp_chair` sits on the 社会民主党 organization observation; the prime-ministership stays in `jp_pm`. 村山富市 was Prime
Minister from June 1994 to January 1996, 土井たか子 Speaker of the House of Representatives from 1993 to 1996, 山花貞夫 a minister
in 1993-1994 and 福島瑞穂 a minister in 2009-2010: only their party-office wording is imported (私は党の委員長であり, 社会党委員長として),
never prime-ministerial, ministerial or Speaker wording. No `jp_pm` claim or source feeds the party role and no party claim or
source feeds `jp_pm`: every claim and source id this packet adds begins `jp_sdp`, and none of `jp_pm`'s or the LDP role's does.
Parliamentary groups (日本社会党・護憲共同, 社会民主党・護憲連合) are not the party office.

The role follows the office named in the handoff across the renaming: 日本社会党's 委員長 (formally 中央執行委員長) to January 1996
and 社会民主党's 党首 after it. That is a scope decision for this role only. The renaming is recorded as claims about the
organization; it does not merge 日本社会党 with this election-list observation, give the observation a lifecycle, or map it. The
November 2020 convention decision that let members either keep the SDP or join 立憲民主党 is likewise a claim only: nothing links
this observation to `jp_sangiin_pr_2025_05` (立憲民主党) or to any game party.

## Observations

### SDP-CHAIR-01 — 土井たか子, the chair when the period opens

Evidence: in the House of Representatives Budget Committee on 6 April 1990, 山口鶴男, whom the same record identifies as the
party's secretary-general (his own account of a 書記長・幹事長会談 with 小沢幹事長, and the Prime Minister's reply to 山口書記長),
says '土井委員長はかねがね強調いたしておられます' (`jp_sdp_doi_named_chair_by_secretary_general_19900406`). On 19 October 1990 he
recalls a party leaders' meeting on 'August 22' attended by '土井委員長、私、大出国対委員長', printing no year
(`jp_sdp_doi_leaders_meeting_recalled_by_secretary_general`). The party's profile of 土井たか子, captured in May 2000, says she became
中央執行委員長 of 日本社会党 in September 1986 and resigned in July 1991 (`jp_sdp_doi_profile_chair_from_september_1986`,
`jp_sdp_doi_profile_resigned_july_1991`).

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 6 April 1990, the first dated statement by a party officer naming her in the office
found after 1 January 1990, with no start or end. Earlier 1990 records name her only in the Prime Minister's replies to her
representative questions, which are not a party officer's statements (leads).

Limits: nothing attests 1 January 1990 itself; no party record or party officer's statement of her 1991 resignation, its acceptance
or an acting chair was found; the profile's months are retrospective.

### SDP-CHAIR-02 — 1991: 田邊誠

Evidence: in the House of Representatives Budget Committee on 20 August 1991, 山花貞夫 introduces himself as having become 書記長
at the recent party convention (さきの党大会で書記長に就任いたしました; a minister's answer calls him 山花書記長) and says
'過日の代表質問で田邊委員長が指摘しましたとおり' (`jp_sdp_tanabe_named_chair_by_secretary_general_19910820`).

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 20 August 1991, with no start or end. The Prime Ministers' replies of 7 August
1991 and 4 November 1992 addressing 田邊委員長 are leads.

Limits: the convention (さきの党大会) is not dated here; neither 田邊誠's selection nor his resignation (December 1992 to January
1993) is attested by a party record or a party officer's dated statement; 土井たか子's end is not stated.

### SDP-CHAIR-03 — January 1993: 山花貞夫

Evidence: 山花貞夫 opens his representative question of 25 January 1993 with '新しく日本社会党の委員長に就任いたしました山花貞夫であります'
(`jp_sdp_yamahana_self_stated_chair_19930125`). As a minister answering in the House of Councillors on 24 September 1993, he says
he is still chair ('なお日本社会党の委員長でございます'; `jp_sdp_yamahana_still_chair_19930924`).

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 25 January 1993; 就任 without a day is not a start. The 24 September 1993
statement is a continuation (party-office wording only; the ministerial office is excluded).

Limits: his January 1993 election is not dated. His resignation fell between 24 September and early October 1993, but his
statements of 7 October 1993 that he had resigned (委員長を辞任いたしまして) and referring to 村山新委員長 were made as a minister
holding no party office (幹部の席にはございません), so they are leads, as CLAUDE-C01-18 treated 河野洋平's statements as foreign
minister and former president; no end is stated.

### SDP-CHAIR-04 — September 1993: 村山富市 as chair

Evidence: 村山富市 made one Diet speech between his election and becoming Prime Minister (a representative question of 12 May
1994, answered 村山委員長にお答えを申し上げます by the Prime Minister: a lead). As Prime Minister he states the party office himself:
'私は党の委員長であり' on 13 October 1994 (`jp_sdp_murayama_self_stated_chair_19941013`), '私は党の委員長として' on 18 October 1994,
'私は当時社会党の委員長を、今もそうですけれども' on 6 February 1995, 'これは社会党委員長としても同じであります' on 19 May 1995 and
'私が党の委員長として' twice on 11 October 1995 (five continuation claims). The party's 2025 statement on his death lists
中央執行委員長 among his party posts (`jp_sdp_murayama_obituary_chair_retrospective`).

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 13 October 1994, the first dated statement by him or a party officer naming him in
the office after his September 1993 election; only party-office wording is imported.

Limits: his September 1993 election is not attested by a party record or a party officer's dated statement; no start or end.

### SDP-CHAIR-05 — January 1996: the renaming and the 党首

Evidence: on 24 January 1996, 佐藤観樹 opens his representative question as the newly appointed 幹事長 of 社会民主党
(`jp_sdp_renamed_party_named_by_secretary_general_19960124`): the new name is in use in a party officer's statement. The party's
statement on its twentieth anniversary (19 January 2016) says 日本社会党 changed its name to 社会民主党 on 1996年1月19日
(`jp_sdp_renaming_retrospective_anniversary_statement`); its 2025 statement says the name was changed in January 1996 and that he
became the first 党首 at the first SDP convention in March of that year (`jp_sdp_murayama_obituary_renaming_retrospective`,
`jp_sdp_murayama_obituary_first_party_leader_retrospective`).

Decision: accepted in part, for the organization claims only. No holder observation of 村山富市 as 党首 is added: he made no Diet
speech in 1996, the 1996 references to 村山党首 found are by members whom the records do not identify as party officers or by
other parties, and the party's archived site begins in late 1999.

Limits: the renaming decision and its taking effect cannot be separated with the sources reviewed; whether 党首 dates from the
January renaming or from a March 1996 convention is unresolved; no contemporaneous record of the 64th convention was found (its
archived pages are 404 captures).

### SDP-CHAIR-06 — September 1996 to November 2003: 土井たか子 as 党首

Evidence: the party's report of its 都道府県連合幹事長会議 of 30 November 1996 says the opening address was given by 土井たか子党首
(`jp_sdp_doi_in_office_kanjicho_meeting_19961130`) and that 伊藤幹事長 reported on the 土井党首就任 amid the dissolution emergency
(`jp_sdp_doi_assumption_reported_to_meeting_19961130`); at the second extraordinary convention of 22 December 1996 she says she has
just been confirmed as party leader (`jp_sdp_doi_convention_approval_19961222`). 社会新報 of 21 January 1998 reports the re-notice
of the leader election on the 8th, the confirmation of her uncontested election on the 10th and calls her 再選の土井党首
(`jp_sdp_doi_leader_renotice_19980108`, `jp_sdp_doi_reelection_confirmed_19980110`, `jp_sdp_doi_in_office_shakaishinpo_19980121`); her
address of 30 August 1998 speaks of 党首たる私. Her statement of 21 January 2000 on her third election is signed 社会民主党 党首
土井たか子 (`jp_sdp_doi_in_office_third_term_danwa_20000121`), and 社会新報 of 26 January 2000 reports the declaration of that day
(`jp_sdp_doi_third_election_declared_20000121`). On 13 November 2003 she announced she would resign and said the standing committee
had assented that day (`jp_sdp_doi_resignation_intent_20031113`, `jp_sdp_doi_resignation_accepted_standing_committee_20031113`); on 15
November she stated her resignation to the representatives' meeting (`jp_sdp_doi_resignation_submitted_20031115`), recalling the
three-officer meeting on the 11th and the committee on the 13th, and that in 1996 she took over from 村山富市前党首
(`jp_sdp_murayama_predecessor_recalled`). 福島瑞穂 refers to 土井たか子前党首 on 13 December 2003 and to her leadership from September
1996 (`jp_sdp_doi_former_leader_referred_20031213`, `jp_sdp_doi_term_span_recalled`). The party's 2025 statement says 村山富市 passed
the post to 土井たか子 at the December 1996 convention (`jp_sdp_murayama_obituary_handover_to_doi_retrospective`).

Decision: accepted in part: three observations, 30 November 1996, 21 January 1998 and 21 January 2000, with no start or end.

Limits: the day of her succession and of 村山富市's departure is not stated, and the retrospective accounts conflict (1996;
September 1996; the December 1996 convention, against the 30 November record that already calls her 党首); no 2002 election record
was found; her resignation steps conflict on the day of the three-officer meeting (13 November in the press conference, 11
November in her address); no source states the day her office ended.

### SDP-CHAIR-07 — November 2003 to July 2013: 福島瑞穂

Evidence: the joint plenary meeting of the party's Diet members chose her as new leader on 15 November 2003, as she and 土井たか子
told the representatives' meeting that day (`jp_sdp_fukushima_joint_plenary_selection_20031115`,
`jp_sdp_fukushima_selection_reported_by_doi_20031115`); the party records her 新党首就任あいさつ of that day
(`jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_inaugural_greeting_20031115`). In the House of Councillors on 26 November 2003 she says
'私は、十一月十五日、社民党の新党首になりました' (`jp_sdp_fukushima_assumption_stated_20031115`). The convention confirmed her on 13
December 2003. 社会新報 reports her uncontested fourth election on 4 December 2009 and fifth on 20 January 2012, and names 福島党首 at
press conferences on 9 December 2009 and 24 January 2012 (`jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_20091209`, `jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_20120124`).
The party's page of 25 July 2013 reports that she stated her intention to resign at the standing committee, which accepted it and
decided to appoint an acting leader, and quotes her press conference: 'その敗北の責任を取って本日で辞任する'
(`jp_sdp_fukushima_end_stated_20130725`); it adds that she was re-elected unopposed five times after 2003.

Decision: accepted in part: from 15 November 2003 (her own statement of the day); observations of 9 December 2009 and 24 January
2012; until 25 July 2013 on the 2012 observation.

Limits: the 2005 and 2007 elections were not found (the page of 2013 counts five uncontested re-elections); the 2003 and 2009
observations have no end, and no end is inferred from the next election.

### SDP-CHAIR-08 — 2013-2018: the acting leader and 吉田忠智

Evidence: the standing committee designated 又市征治幹事長 as acting leader on 1 August 2013
(`jp_sdp_mataichi_acting_designated_20130801`), decided on 29 August to hold a leader election, and set its schedule on 5
September; the election was announced on 27 September with two candidates, and the 14 October count gave 吉田忠智 9,986 votes and
石川大我 2,239 (`jp_sdp_leader_election_2013_count_20131014`, `jp_sdp_yoshida_result_declared_20131014`). In the House of Councillors on
24 October 2013 吉田忠智 says '党首になりまして今日は初めての質問であります' (`jp_sdp_yoshida_in_office_diet_20131024`). His 2015 intention to
stand again, his July 2016 resignation intention and its withdrawal on 1 September 2016 are claims; at the convention of 24
February 2018 he recalls that he took over from 福島みずほ前党首 on 1 November 2013 (`jp_sdp_yoshida_takeover_recalled_20131101`) and
explains his departure.

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 24 October 2013; the acting leader is never a holder.

Limits: the 1 November 2013 takeover he recalls conflicts with his 24 October statement and is never a start (the 2013 procedure
had the national representatives' meeting of 26 October approve the new leader serving the rest of the term); the 2015 result was
not found; no source states the day his office ended in 2018.

### SDP-CHAIR-09 — 2018-2020: 又市征治

Evidence: the leader election announced on 12 January 2018 drew no candidate; on the re-notice of 26 January 又市征治 was elected
unopposed, the party saying he would formally take office at the late-February convention (`jp_sdp_mataichi_uncontested_election_20180126`).
His address of 25 February 2018 is headed 党首就任あいさつ and signed 社会民主党党首 又市征治 (`jp_sdp_mataichi_in_office_inaugural_greeting_20180225`);
he says in the Diet on 2 March 2018 that he recently took office; the party's report of the convention of 22-23 February 2020 names
又市党首 giving the opening address.

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 25 February 2018, with no start or end.

Limits: the 2020 records give no resignation, end day or reason for his departure (the party's February 2020 statements were not
archived).

### SDP-CHAIR-10 — 2020-2023: 福島瑞穂 again

Evidence: the party's news items dated 2020.2.28 report that at the 17th convention of 22-23 February 2020 福島みずほ副党首 was chosen
unopposed as new leader in the leader election of the first day (`jp_sdp_fukushima_uncontested_election_20200222`), call her
福島みずほ新党首 and print her address as new leader (`jp_sdp_fukushima_new_leader_news_item_20200228`). The standing committee fixed on
22 October 2020 the motion for the 14 November extraordinary convention, which passed by majority that members mutually accept both
keeping the SDP and joining 立憲民主党 (`jp_sdp_convention_agenda_decided_20201022`, `jp_sdp_split_decision_20201114`). The party's notice
of 14 January 2022 reports the notice, her filing, her uncontested re-election and 福島党首's press conference
(`jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_20220114`); 社会新報 of 1 December 2023 reports her uncontested re-election that day with a caption naming
福島党首 (`jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_20231201`).

Decision: accepted in part: three observations, 28 February 2020, 14 January 2022 and 1 December 2023, with no start or end. The
2020 convention decision is a claim about the organization only.

Limits: her address at the end of the 2020 convention is not dated; the 2022 notice says the new term would begin after the March
2022 convention (a plan, not a start).

### SDP-CHAIR-11 — 2026: the contested leader election

Evidence: the election headquarters moved the notice day on 18 February 2026; the election was announced on 4 March with three
candidates (大椿ゆうこ, ラサール石井 and 福島みずほ党首, who is attested as incumbent that day); the 23 March count gave no candidate a
majority (福島みずほ 1,876, 大椿ゆうこ 1,297, ラサール石井 967) and a run-off between the top two was announced the same day
(`jp_sdp_leader_election_2026_first_round_count_20260323`, `jp_sdp_leader_election_2026_renotice_20260323`); the 6 April count gave
福島みずほ 2,364 and 大椿ゆうこ 1,792 and named her the winner (`jp_sdp_leader_election_2026_rerun_count_20260406`,
`jp_sdp_fukushima_elected_2026_20260406`). 社会新報 reports 社民党の福島みずほ党首's press conference of 8 April 2026
(`jp_sdp_fukushima_in_office_kaiken_20260408`), her address to the 21st convention on 29 April and her press conference of 29 July
2026.

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 8 April 2026, with no start; 29 April and 29 July 2026 are continuations before the
cutoff.

Limits: the day the new term began is not stated (the 21st convention of 28-29 April 2026 is not recorded as a start); the
deputy leaders named in these records hold other offices and are not imported.

hash the bytes as received. No capture was served gzip-encoded; every recorded identity is the identity-encoded body.

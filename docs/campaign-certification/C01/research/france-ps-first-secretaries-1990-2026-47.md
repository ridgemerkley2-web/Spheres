### How a start and an end are decided

- A party record that styles, lists or signs a person as Premier secrétaire gives `attested_on` only. Where the record prints no
  event day, the party weekly's printed issue date dates it (C01-29, C01-31); a relative day ('jeudi', 'samedi') resolves from the
  dated issue that prints it (C01-29, C01-39).
- An election by the comité directeur, the congress, the Conseil national or the members, a vote result, a ratification and a new
  holder's acceptance address are claims, not starts (C01-27, C01-40; Codex's C01-31 review 988d5379). The 'Premier secrétaire élu'
  styling of 29 March 2018 is an election claim.
- No reviewed party record states the day any of the ten took up the office, so no `from` is recorded.
- Departures are claims: Pierre Mauroy 'quitte ses fonctions' (no day); Laurent Fabius 'a démissionné' at the comité directeur of
  3 April 1993 (no day or time of acceptance or effect); the Conseil national's no-confidence vote in Michel Rocard of 19 June 1994;
  François Hollande's farewell of 19 November 2008; Harlem Désir's departure for the government (undated); Jean-Christophe
  Cambadélis's decision to hand over (no day) and the 'ancien Premier secrétaire' styling of 24 June 2017. None states a resignation
  accepted or effective on a stated day (C01-29, C01-36), so no `until` is recorded, and no successor's election is used as an end.
- Acting, delegated, provisional and collective arrangements are claims only (C01-22, C01-34).
- Retrospective party pages are claims only, and a CMS publication stamp is never an attestation date (C01-32).

## Observations

### FR-PS-01 — Pierre Mauroy

In office on 1 January 1990. Vendredi n°54 (24 March 1990) reports his re-election 'à l'unanimité par le comité directeur' after
the Rennes congress, without a day, and lists him first in the new secrétariat national. His declaration of 7 January 1992 to the
bureau exécutif, printed in Vendredi n°127, places his mandate at the disposal of the next comité directeur and recalls his
election of 14 May 1988; the same issue says he 'quitte ses fonctions' without a day. Two dated observations; no boundary.

### FR-PS-02 — Laurent Fabius

Vendredi n°127 (10 January 1992) reports his election by the comité directeur on 'jeudi' (9 January 1992), prints his acceptance
address and styles him premier secrétaire. Vendredi n°183 (9 April 1993) reports that, put in the minority at the comité directeur
of 3 April 1993, he resigned; it states no day or time of acceptance or effect. One dated observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-03 — Michel Rocard

Vendredi n°183 records the provisional direction installed on 6 April 1993 under his presidency (claims only: it is not the office
of Premier secrétaire). The congress edition of 24 October 1993 gives the result of the first secret-ballot election of the
premier secrétaire by the Le Bourget congress on 'samedi' (23 October): 475 votes, 80,92 %. Vendredi n°207 (12 November 1993)
lists and styles him premier secrétaire. Vendredi n°234 (24 June 1994) reports the Conseil national's no-confidence vote of
19 June 1994 and the 'direction démissionnaire'; no day of his leaving office is stated. One dated observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-04 — Henri Emmanuelli

Vendredi n°234 (24 June 1994): 'Le Conseil National du 19 juin a élu Henri Emmanuelli premier secrétaire du Parti socialiste',
and the same issue styles him 'le premier secrétaire'. The Liévin congress and his departure in October 1995 were not retrieved
in a party record that dates them. One dated observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-05 — Lionel Jospin

Vendredi n°261 (20 October 1995) prints the members' vote (94,16 % yes to his becoming Premier secrétaire; no vote day printed),
the secrétariat national the conseil national approved, opening 'Lionel JOSPIN, Premier secrétaire', and a meeting of Wednesday
18 October 1995 at which he is styled Premier secrétaire. L'Hebdo n°23 (6 June 1997) has François Hollande state that Lionel
Jospin remains Premier secrétaire until the next congress. Two dated observations; no boundary; his government office is not
recorded on this role.

### FR-PS-06 — François Hollande

L'Hebdo n°23 records his delegated office of Premier secrétaire délégué, adopted by the Bureau national on an unprinted day
(claims only). L'Hebdo n°41 prints the members' ballot of Thursday 27 November 1997 with two candidacies; n°43 (5 December 1997)
prints the result (91,18 %), a communiqué headed with his title and the new secrétariat national. L'Hebdo n°508 reports his
farewell as 'ex-Premier secrétaire' on 19 November 2008, which is not a stated end. One dated observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-07 — Martine Aubry

L'Hebdo n°508 (29 November 2008) reports the commission de récolement's count approved at the Conseil national of 25 November
(67 451 to 67 349 votes) and her election declared 'à l'issue du Conseil national du 25 novembre', and styles her Première
secrétaire. Her withdrawal for the 2011 primary and her departure in 2012 were not retrieved in a dated party record. One dated
observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-08 — Harlem Désir

L'Hebdo n°667 calls the members' vote of 18 October 2012 (the issue predates it; no result). L'Hebdo n°677 reports that at Jarnac
on 8 January 2013 'Harlem Désir, Premier secrétaire du PS' led the delegation. L'Hebdo n°730-731 records, without a day, that he
left for the government after eighteen months. No interim service in 2011 was found in the party records retrieved. One dated
observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-09 — Jean-Christophe Cambadélis

L'Hebdo n°730-731 reports his election by the Conseil national on Tuesday 15 April 2014 (67,12 %) and opens the new secrétariat
national with him. L'Hebdo n°867 reports, without a day, his decision to hand over to a collective direction; n°868/869 calls him
'l'ancien Premier secrétaire' at the Conseil national of 24 June 2017, which does not state when his office ended. One dated
observation; no boundary.

### FR-PS-10 — The collective interim, June 2017 to March 2018

The Bureau national of 20 June 2017 deferred the composition of the collegial direction to the Conseil national of 8 July; on
24 June Rachid Temal spoke as secrétaire national for coordination; on 16 March 2018 he signed as 'Coordinateur national du Parti
socialiste'. All are claims only; no holder is recorded for the interim, and its absence from `holder_claims` is deliberate.

### FR-PS-11 — Olivier Faure

The party's statement of 16 March 2018 gives the orientation-text vote of 15 March (his text 48,56 %); its press-conference page
calls him 'Premier secrétaire élu' after the vote of 29 March 2018 (an election claim). A statement of 9 July 2018 is signed as
Premier secrétaire. The communiqués of 20 and 22 January 2023 give the members' vote of 19 January and the reviewed count
(51,09 %); a page on the Conseil national of 11 March 2023 styles him 'notre Premier secrétaire'. The 2025 communiqué reports the
ratification of the votes of 27 May and 5 June 2025 (51,15 %) without printing the ratification day. The party's page 'Le premier
secrétaire' (captured 26 June 2026) dates his first election to 15 March 2018 and lists re-elections in 2021, 2023 and 2025:
claims only, and the 15/29 March discrepancy is kept. Two dated observations; no boundary.

## Decisions for Codex

1. **Importer change.** The supplement could not attach a role to a CNCCFP organization, so `import_cnccfp_census.py` gains the
   optional key `organization_roles` (append-only, one entry per existing organization, citing only its own supplement sources and
   their claims). `packet_check.py` reports this file as outside the packet's allowed paths; the packet's scope ('through
   supplements/france.json and the importer') required it. A ruling may prefer another mechanism.
2. **archives-socialistes.fr PDFs.** The viewer's `allowDownload: false` was treated as a display preference, not an access control,
   because the same public URL is what the viewer fetches without any login, cookie or token. A ruling may instead require citing
   only the ARK permalink, or recording these as blocked.
3. **Resignations and the no-confidence vote.** Laurent Fabius 'a démissionné' at the comité directeur of 3 April 1993, and the
   Conseil national voted its no-confidence in Michel Rocard on 19 June 1994 with a 'direction démissionnaire'; neither states a
   resignation accepted or effective on a stated day, so neither gives `until`. A ruling could treat either as a stated end.
4. **Elections by a competent body on a stated day** (Rocard by the congress on 23 October 1993, Emmanuelli by the Conseil national
   on 19 June 1994, Cambadélis on 15 April 2014) are kept as claims, not `from`, because no record says the office began that day.
5. **Issue dates.** Holders whose record prints no event day are dated by the party weekly's printed issue date (C01-29, C01-31).
   The 1997 communiqué headed with Hollande's title but referring to 25 November (before the 27 November ballot) is dated by the
   issue (5 December 1997), not by its text.
6. **Jospin, June 1997.** François Hollande's statement that Lionel Jospin 'reste Premier Secrétaire' is used as a party officer's
   dated statement (attested_on 1997-06-06); Hollande's own delegated office is claims only.
7. **The 2025 ratification claim** is dated by the 5 June 2025 vote it says re-elected him; the ratification day is not printed.

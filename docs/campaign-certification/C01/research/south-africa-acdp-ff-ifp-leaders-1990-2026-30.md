### How a start and an end are decided

The packet applies the CLAUDE-C01-09 and CLAUDE-C01-16 rule unchanged. A holder has `from` only where a source states the
day the office was assumed or took effect, and `until` only where a source states the day it ended. No record of the
three parties reviewed states either, so every holder is a dated observation, one per reviewed observation, citing only
in-office attestations made on its own `attested_on` day; the test pins that every cited claim carries exactly the
holder's date. Everything else is a claim that never feeds a holder: elections and election references, the IFP's 2019
result publication, selection by congress, acceptance, re-election publication, nomination references, announcements
not to stand again, valedictory and retirement statements, the President Emeritus title, deaths, the parliamentary
leader designation, conference spans and scheduling decisions, predecessor references and continuation attestations.

No end is inferred from a successor's election, designation or first attestation. Three printed days are recorded and
explicitly not used as boundaries: the IFP timeline's "24 August" (2019 block) "Buthelezi officially retires from the
presidency of the Party" (a retrospective list entry); the IFP release of 25 August 2019 whose "last night" would place
Hlabisa's election on 24 August 2019 (derived, not stored); and the Freedom Front Plus's "vandag aangewys" of 12 November
2016 (a designation that states no day of taking office). Retrospective history pages, biographies and curricula vitae
carry no structured date (their printed words are kept as `printed_range` text in the extract rows). Two ACDP holder
days rest on the day a page was served rather than a dateline: the 1999 home page (its archived response Date header is
1 May 1999, and its body text "Voting Day is 32 days away!" was generated for that request) and, as a continuation claim
only, the 2019 leadership page.

### Organizations: founding, renaming and mergers

Founding, renaming, merger and joining claims are organization claims: they sit on the organization observation (role
`null` in the extract rows), not on the role, and set no lifecycle start or end, identity or game mapping. The ACDP's
pages give "December 1993" for its launch; the Freedom Front's give "March 1994"/"Maart 1994"; the 27 September 2003
announcement said the names KP, AEB and VF would "verdwyn" and be replaced by VF+, with no day of legal effect; the

Referendum Party decided to dissolve and join the Freedom Front Plus. None of these merges an identity: the Freedom
Front and the Freedom Front Plus are one role on the VF PLUS observation because the handoff places the Freedom Front's
leader there, and the rows before the announcement carry the title as printed, Leader of the Freedom Front.

## Observations

### ZA-ACDP-01 — The ACDP's founding and its President when the record opens

Evidence: the ACDP's history page (captured 13 January 2004; Last-Modified 11 November 2003) says Christians were
prompted "to launch a new political party in December 1993", so that the party "was established exactly a hundred days
prior to South Africa's first democratic elections in 1994" (`za_acdp_history_party_launched_december_1993`). The ACDP's
2021 profile says "Rev Meshoe founded the African Christian Democratic Party in December 1993"
(`za_acdp_profile_meshoe_founded_party_december_1993`). The ACDP KwaZulu-Natal newsletter headed "May 1998 Vol 1 No 3"
carries "A word from our President (Rev Dr Kenneth Meshoe M.P. Leader of the A.C.D.P)"
(`za_acdp_kzn_newsletter_word_from_president_meshoe_199805`). The ACDP home page as served on 1 May 1999, which counts
down "Voting Day is 32 days away!", presents "Rev Kenneth Meshoe, MP (President ACDP)"
(`za_acdp_home_page_president_meshoe_19990501`).

Decision: accepted in part. Meshoe's first holder is dated 1 May 1999, the earliest ACDP record with a day. The page has
no dateline, so the day is the day it was served (archived Date header), which its generated countdown confirms. The
two founding claims are organization claims with no structured date; founding the party is not a start of the
President's office. The newsletter is month-only and dates nothing.

Limits: no ACDP record from 1993 to April 1998 was found, and none states how or when he became President; the
"hundred days" wording would give a different day from December 1993 and is not used.

## Response identities and stability checks

Codex re-downloads every recorded response and compares its byte count and SHA-256, so each extract carries the recipe
the identities depend on: fetch the recorded `url` exactly, with **no `Accept-Encoding` request header and no automatic
decoding** (for example `curl -s -o FILE URL`, not `--compressed`), and hash the bytes as received. Two captures are
stored and served gzip-encoded even to a request without `Accept-Encoding`, and their extracts record the decoded
identity beside the served one: `za_ifp_statement_passing_buthelezi_20230909` (100,551 bytes decoded) and
`za_ifp_rally_advisory_abaqulusi_20260201` (138,887 bytes decoded). Every extract records the base32 SHA-1 of the bytes;
for 36 captures it equals the Internet Archive's CDX digest. Three do not, with the bytes stable across downloads:
`za_ifp_historical_perspective_page_2000` (an early ARC record; the body length equals the archived original
Content-Length), and `za_ifp_elective_conference_resolutions_2012` and `za_ff_viljoen_media_release_19970826` (archived
from chunked responses, so the digest covers the stored payload).

Stability: every response was downloaded at least twice, the last at least 30 minutes after the first, with the same
byte count and SHA-256; each extract's `stability_check` gives the times. The ACDP sources were downloaded at 17:20-17:28Z
and again at 17:58-17:59Z on 28 September 2026. The Freedom Front and IFP sources were downloaded by the helpers
(17:22-18:08Z) and again 30-64 minutes later, and every one was downloaded a third time by the packet author (18:50-19:05Z)
with the same result. The replay of the 25 March 2026 release is the exception that changed the source: the capture
first used (19:09 UTC) began to redirect to a second capture of the same page (20:23 UTC) on 28 September 2026, and
that second capture serves identical bytes (its CDX digest equals theirs); the source now records the second capture,
downloaded at 19:17Z and again at 19:48Z with the same result.

Per-request traps: no response is generated per request. The 1999 ACDP home page is an ASP page whose countdown was
generated when the archive captured it; the archive replays those fixed bytes. No search, feed, tag, category, listing
or cache-busting URL is used, and no PDF is used. The test pins every URL to the raw `id_` form on web.archive.org of the
three parties' own hosts and rejects search, API, feed, listing and cache-busting URL shapes.

### How a holder is dated

The packet follows the rule of CLAUDE-C01-18 and CLAUDE-C01-29. A holder has `from` only where a source states the day office was
assumed, and `until` only where a source states the day it ended; otherwise it has `attested_on`. One holder observation is kept
per selection or re-election found, so 神崎武法's re-elections of 2002 and 2004 and 山口那津男's of 2012 to 2022 have their own
observations.

- **What dates an observation.** A same-day party record naming the person as 委員長 or 代表 after the selection (the party organ's
  report of the convention, the heading and text of the new leader's address), or, in the Diet minutes, a party officer's own
  statement: the leader's own statement of the office, or another officer's statement naming the leader where the record
  identifies him as a party officer (市川雄一 as 市川書記長 in 1993). Where a party page prints no day for the event it reports, the
  observation is dated by the page's own date line (the 1998 and 2002 conventions). Each holder cites only such claims, each
  carrying exactly the holder's date.
- **What states a start.** Only a new holder's own first-person statement, on a day the source prints, that he or she takes office
  now: 太田昭宏's '本日の党大会において…公明党代表に就任させていただきました', introduced as the address at the convention of the 30th,
  and 竹谷とし子's 'ただいま皆さまのご信任を賜り、公明党代表の大任を担わせていただくことになりました', captioned 14日 (as CLAUDE-C01-18 read
  森喜朗's 'ただ今…就任致すことになりました'). A statement of being chosen (選出されました, 選んでいただきました, 選任されました), a greeting on
  taking office (代表就任にあたり) and a re-elected leader's statement that he continues (引き続き, 再び…重責を務めさせていただく) date an
  observation but are not read as a start.
- **What never feeds a holder.** Convention selections and re-elections, the national meeting of representatives' selection,
  recommendations by the central executive committee, recollections of a selection, announced resignations, predecessor references
  (前代表), a recalled vacancy, continuation attestations, the 1994 acting chair (委員長代行), the 代表代理 of January-March 2026 and
  its styled title, other organizations' offices, the organization claims and every retrospective record. Party rules are procedure
  only, never a date.
- **No end.** No source reviewed states the day any leader's office ended. The December 1994 dissolution is recorded only
  retrospectively; 石井啓一's announced resignation, the 前代表 references and the vacancy of January 2026 are claims; no end is
  inferred from a successor's selection.

Names follow the sources in Japanese, one holder string per person (石田幸四郎, 神崎武法, 太田昭宏, 山口那津男, 石井啓一, 斉藤鉄夫, 竹谷とし子). Where a source prints a surname and title (石田委員長,
神崎代表, 太田代表, 山口代表, 石井代表, 斉藤代表, 竹谷代表) the claim text keeps that form. A row's `holder_name` is null where the source
names no holder of this office: an organization event, another organization's office, the acting chair or the interim 代表代理.
Romanized forms: 石田幸四郎 Ishida Kōshirō, 神崎武法 Kanzaki Takenori, 太田昭宏 Ōta Akihiro, 山口那津男 Yamaguchi Natsuo, 石井啓一 Ishii

## Observations

### KOMEITO-01 — 石田幸四郎, the chair when the period opens

Evidence: in the House of Representatives Budget Committee on 29 January 1993, 市川雄一, whom the Prime Minister and a minister
address as 市川書記長 in the same record, says '先日、二十五日、我が党の石田委員長の代表質問における借り上げ住宅の提案に対して'. Answering as
総務庁長官 on 5 November 1993, 石田幸四郎 recalls taking office as chair in May 1989 after 矢野前委員長; on 16 June 1994 he says that
the party has placed an acting chair (委員長代行) while he serves in the cabinet but '私が委員長であることに変わりはありません'. The party's
2003 history calls him 石田幸四郎委員長（当時）.

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 29 January 1993, the first dated statement by a party officer naming him in the office
found after 1 January 1990. The 1994 statement is a continuation; the acting chair is a claim only.

Limits: nothing attests 1 January 1990 itself; his 1990-1992 speeches speak for the parliamentary group, and the 1990-1992 records
naming 石田委員長 are the Prime Minister's replies or remarks by members whom the records do not identify as party officers (leads).
No contemporaneous party record of 1990-1994 was found online, and no source states the day his office ended.

### KOMEITO-02 — December 1994 to 1998: the division and the organizations in between

Evidence: the English page of 公明 (captured 2 February 1998) says 'We formed Komei on December 5, 1994' and lists its representative
from that day; the party's history page (captured 6 February 2003) records the old party's final convention on 5 December 1994,
its dissolution (党の解散) and the launch the same day of 公明 and 公明新党, which joined the founding of the 新進党 on 10 December 1994;
the 新進党's dissolution on 27 December 1997; and the formation of 黎明クラブ (Councillors, on the premise of joining 公明) and 新党平和
(most Representatives), 黎明クラブ merging into 公明 three weeks later.

Decision: accepted in part, for organization claims only; no holder of this role is observed between the division and November
1998, and 公明's representative is recorded as another office.

Limits: every account is retrospective; the days of 黎明クラブ's and 新党平和's formation and of their leaders' offices are not
printed; 公明's own English history page dates 公明新党's formation to 10 December 1994 against the 2003 history's same-day launch
(a lead, not imported).

### KOMEITO-03 — 7 November 1998: the new 公明党 and 神崎武法

Evidence: the party organ's report of 8 November 1998 (copied to the party's site) says the new party took the form of 新党平和
dissolving and merging into 公明 as the surviving party, that 公明's expanded central committee decided the name 「公明党」 and the rules
on 24 October, that the convention confirmed (信任) 神崎代表, 浜四津代表代行 and the other officers by show of hands, and that 神崎代表
spoke as a new officer. In the House of Representatives on 30 November 1998 神崎武法 says the merger took place at the national
convention of 7 November.

Decision: accepted in part. The holder is dated 8 November 1998 by the report's own date line; the convention's day is printed only
in the Diet statement, which is used as a claim about the organizations (the record identifies him only as speaking for his group).

Limits: the page gives no day for the convention; no statement of the day he assumed office was found.

### KOMEITO-04 — 神崎武法's re-elections, 2000-2004

Evidence: the party's page dated 3 November 2002 reports that the election administrator announced 神崎代表 as the only candidate and
that the convention re-appointed him, naming '新代表に再任された神崎代表'; his address of the same date line says he continues as party
representative by the delegates' confidence. The party organ of 1 November 2004 introduces the address of '３１日に開かれた党全国大会で再任
された神崎武法代表', who says he will again bear the duty of party representative. 太田昭宏 thanks 神崎武法前代表 for four terms and eight
years on 30 September 2006.

Decision: accepted in part: observations of 3 November 2002 and 31 October 2004, with no start or end.

Limits: the 2000 re-election was not found; the 2002 convention's day is not printed; no source states the day his office ended.

### KOMEITO-05 — 2006-2009: 太田昭宏

Evidence: the party organ of 1 October 2006 reports that on the 30th the sixth convention elected 太田昭宏 as the only candidate by the

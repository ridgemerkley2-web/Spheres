## Observations

### SA-CHR-01 — The Shura Council Law of 1992 and the council's formation (procedure only)

Evidence:

- The Bureau of Experts' Arabic law record, as captured in May 2025 (`sa_shura_law_a91_dates_19920301`): Royal Order A/91
  of 27/8/1412 AH, issue date 1 March 1992, publication date 6 March 1992, in force. The order's clauses
  (`sa_shura_law_a91_order_terms`) replace the 1347 AH law and require publication in the gazette and application within
  six months.
- Articles 3, 10, 11 and 13 (`sa_shura_law_art3_original_60`, `sa_shura_law_art10_chair_by_royal_order`,
  `sa_shura_law_art11_oath_before_work`, `sa_shura_law_art13_term`): originally a Chairman and sixty members; the Chairman
  is appointed and relieved by royal order; he swears the oath before the King before starting work; each term is four
  Hijri years from the date specified in the forming order.
- The Bureau's English translation cover prints "Royal Decree No. A/91, March 2, 1992" (`sa_shura_law_en_cover_19920302`).
  The Council's own 2001 copy of the law prints only the Hijri date and the 120-member Article 3 of 2001
  (`sa_shura_site_law_a91_27_8_1412`, `sa_shura_site_law_art3_120_2001`).
- The Council's histories and statistics: the King's speech of 27/8/1412, the internal rules of 3/3/1414, the first session
  on 29 December 1993, the 90-member council of 5 July 1997, and the count starts 20/7/1414, 3/3/1418 and 3/3/1422
  (`sa_shura_history_fahd_speech_27_8_1412`, `sa_shura_history_internal_rules_3_3_1414`,
  `sa_shura_first_session_inaugurated_19931229`, `sa_shura_membership_90_19970705`, `sa_shura_term_years_listed`).

Decision: accepted as procedure and claims only. No holder, `from` or `until` is derived from a law text, a history or a
statistics index.

Limits: the Arabic record gives 1 March 1992 and the English cover 2 March 1992; the Royal Embassy's 1997 release agrees
with the Arabic record. The 1418 AH amendment of Article 3 is A/62 in the 1422 AH orders and A/63 in the 1997 release. The
gazette issue was not read.

### SA-CHR-02 — The first Chairman's appointment

Evidence:

- `sa_jubair_speech_first_term_opening`: the Council's archive of speeches for the first year of the first term (1414-1415
  AH) prints the speech of "the Chairman of the Shura Council, Sheikh محمد بن ابراهيم بن جبير", which ends by inviting the
  King to deliver the address opening the Council's work in its new form; the King's address then says "we open today the
  Shura Council in its new framework". The dossier had attributed that sentence to the Chairman; it is the King's.
- `sa_jubair_dates_first_term_opening_16_7_1414`: in his speech at the second term's opening he dates the first council's
  opening to 16 Rajab 1414 AH; the Council's English page gives 29 December 1993.
- `sa_first_council_a16_cited_19930820`: the 1997 A/72 release says the first council had been formed by "royal decree
  number A/16 dated 3/3/1414 H (August 20, 1993)".

Decision: accepted in part. Jubair is Chairman at the first term's opening by the Council's own record, but that record is a
2009 publication with no day, so it is a claim, not his `attested_on`. No order of his first appointment was read, so his
`from` is null.

Limits: A/16 of 3/3/1414 was not read, nor A/14 of the same day, which A/79 (2001) and A/13 (2009) cite when appointing
the Vice Chairman and the Chairman at ministerial rank and which may be the first Chairman's appointment. Umm al-Qura issues
of 1412-1422 AH are not online and the old SPA archive has no captures from before about 2001.

### SA-CHR-03 — Reappointments, the end of the first chairmanship and the successor

Evidence for 1997 (Royal Embassy releases of 5, 6 and 14 July 1997):

- `sa_jubair_first_term_end_19970706`: King Fahd's letter to "Chairman of the Shura Council Sheikh Muhammad Ibn Ibrahim
  Ibn Jubair" on the end of the first term, and the Chairman's last meeting of that term, on 6 July 1997. This is the
  earliest dated attestation of him as Chairman and his holder's `attested_on`.
- `sa_order_a72_second_term_19970706` and `sa_jubair_extension_a90_19970706`: A/72 announces, "effective from 3/3/1418 H
  (July 7, 1997)", the second council with him first "(Chairman of the Shura Council)"; A/90 extends his ministerial-rank
  appointment as Chairman for four years from the same day. Both are continuations: neither is a `from`.
- `sa_jubair_oath_second_term_19970714` and `sa_second_term_inaugurated_19970714`: on 14 July 1997 he and the members swore
  the oath before the King, who then inaugurated the second term. The Council's 2009 page of second-term speeches
  (`sa_jubair_speech_second_term_opening`) matches this ceremony but prints no day, so its period is not changed.

Evidence for 2001:

- `sa_order_a80_third_term_20010524`: Royal Order A/80 of 1/3/1422 AH forms the third council, its list headed "الشيخ محمد بن
  ابراهيم بن جبير / رئيس مجلس الشورى". The Royal Embassy's release of 24 May 2001 (`sa_fahd_message_chair_20010524`) says
  the decrees and the King's message to "Shura Council Chairman Muhammad bin Jubair" were issued that day; the Arabic
  letter itself is undated (`sa_fahd_letter_chair_end_second_term`). A/78 and A/79 of the same day raise the Council to 120
  members and appoint the Vice Chairman from 3/3/1422.
- `sa_jubair_oath_third_term_20010604`, `sa_third_term_start_announced_20010604`: the oath of 4 June 2001, when the King
  announced "the start of the third term". `sa_jubair_chairs_first_session_20010605`: the first session on 5 June "under
  the chairmanship of Shaikh Muhammad bin Jubair, whose term of office has been renewed". SPA copy on the Council's site
  attests him chairing on 10 June and calls the designation an extension on 11 June.

Evidence for 2002:

- `sa_jubair_chairs_48th_session_20020115`: his last dated attestation, 15 January 2002.
- `sa_jubair_death_20020124`: the Royal Embassy's release of 24 January 2002: "The Royal Court has announced that Shaikh
  Muhammad bin Ibrahim bin Jubair passed away this morning"; he "was Chairman ... from its inception until his death". This
  is his `until`. The Council's own statements of the death (`sa_jubair_death_stated_sg_column`, and the successor's words
  in `sa_humaid_chairs_56th_session_20020212`) give no day.
- `sa_vice_chair_presides_20020127`, `sa_vice_chair_presides_55th_20020210`: the Vice Chairman presided on 27 January and
  10 February 2002. Claims only.
- `sa_humaid_appointed_20020207`: "A Royal Decree was issued today announcing the appointment of Dr. Saleh bin Abdullah bin
  Humaid as Chairman" (7 February 2002). SPA itself equates 28/11/1422 with 11 February 2002
  (`sa_calendar_14221128_20020211`), so 7 February is 24/11/1422, the day in his Council CV
  (`sa_humaid_cv_chair_since_24_11_1422`): the CV and the magazine do not conflict, as the dossier had thought.
- `sa_humaid_first_day_20020211`: the Council's magazine: on Monday 28/11/1422 the Council received its Chairman "who began
  his work in the Council as its Chairman", "his first day as Chairman". This is his `from`.
- `sa_humaid_order_issued_before_20020212`, `sa_humaid_oath_before_20020212`, `sa_humaid_chairs_56th_session_20020212`:
  the magazine's account of the 56th session (29/11/1422 = 12 February 2002), the first he chaired after the royal order and
  after taking the oath, kept as three claims. SPA's Arabic items of that day (`sa_spa_humaid_chair_received_20020212`,
  `sa_spa_humaid_addresses_council_20020212`) and its English main story (`sa_spa_en_humaid_first_session_20020212`)
  corroborate the session.

Decision: accepted. Jubair's tenure ends on 24 January 2002 (death while in office). Humaid's starts with the decree of 7
February 2002 (`attested_on`) and his stated first day of 11 February 2002 (`from`); the undated oath is never a `from`.

Limits: the 24 January announcement and the 7 February decree are read in the Royal Embassy's English releases; the Arabic
Royal Court statement and the decree text, number and any effective day were not found (the old SPA archive has no
captures for 16 January to 1 February or 3 to 11 February 2002). If the integrator does not accept the embassy releases as
official Saudi records, Jubair's `until` reverts to null and Humaid's `attested_on` to the next dated record (11 February).

### SA-CHR-04 — The 2009 appointment of a new Chairman

Evidence:

- `sa_royal_orders_issued_20090214` and `sa_alsheikh_appointed_a13_20090214`: Royal Order A/13 dated 19/2/1430 AH, released
  on 14 February 2009, relieves "معالي الشيخ الدكتور عبدالله بن محمد بن إبراهيم آل الشيخ" of the Justice portfolio and
  appoints him Chairman of the Shura Council at ministerial rank.
- `sa_alsheikh_effective_a13_20090228`: item First closes "اعتباراً من 3/3/1430هـ", which covers both the relief and the
  appointment. SPA datelines print 3 Rabi al-Awwal 1430 as 28 February 2009 (`sa_calendar_14300303_20090228`) and 4 Rabi
  al-Awwal 1430 as 1 March 2009 (the oath report). This is his `from`.
- `sa_shura_formed_a15_20090214`: A/15 of the same day forms the new council with him first as Chairman, for four Hijri years
  from the end of the previous council's term (no day printed).
- `sa_alsheikh_oath_20090301`: he and the members took the oath before King Abdullah on 1 March 2009; among those attending
  was "رئيس المجلس الأعلى للقضاء الدكتور صالح بن حميد" (`sa_humaid_styled_sjc_chair_20090301`).
- `sa_shura_cv_a13_retrospective_20260808`: the Council's biography of him cites A/13 of 19/2/1430 (and the Justice order
  2/125 of 19/3/1413); a claim only.

Decision: accepted. His holder is observed on 2009-02-14 with `from` 2009-02-28.

Limits: no source states the end of Humaid's chairmanship. A/13 does not relieve him, and his styling as Chairman of the
Supreme Judicial Council on 1 March 2009 is another office; neither is an `until`. The order that appointed him to that
council (A/5, same day) is a lead. The Gregorian day of 3/3/1430 rests on SPA datelines, not on the order.

## Response identities and stability checks

- **Internet Archive raw captures (32 sources).** Each is a raw `id_` capture made before the cutoff, fetched with curl's
  default User-Agent and `Accept-Encoding: identity`. Thirty were served without `Content-Encoding`; the recorded identity is
  that body. Two captures of the Council's current site (`sa_shura_cv_alsheikh_20260808`, `sa_shura_news_a54_20161202`) are
  of originals that were themselves gzip-encoded, so web.archive.org replays them with `Content-Encoding: gzip` even to
  `Accept-Encoding: identity`. Following the house convention (check defect B5), their recorded identity is the uncompressed
  body (60,974 and 56,416 bytes); the gzip transport bodies (15,780 and 18,610 bytes) are recorded beside them in
  `transport_response`.
- **SPA portal API (39 sources).** SPA's article pages embed a live `views_count` and per-request tokens, so their bytes
  change on every fetch (the C01-06 sources record only an article-content hash for that reason). The portal's single-item
  API response that each page loads, `https://portalapi.spa.gov.sa/api/v1/news/<id>`, carries no counter, token or request
  time, and is recorded instead, with the page as `original_url` and the SHA-256 of `data.content` as a second identity.
  Each was downloaded with curl's default User-Agent (no browser User-Agent), once plainly and once, at least 30 minutes
  later, with a cache-busting query that Cloudflare reported as MISS, so the second body came from the origin, not an edge
  copy. The 2016 and 2020 releases embed their additions in `related_news`; the 2024 and 2026 items list translations, which
  could grow if SPA adds a language version (none changed between 26 and 28 September 2026).
- **Umm al-Qura.** Every response of the live gazette page embeds a request time and a fresh CSRF token (check defect B6), so
  the source is recorded through its raw capture of 14 September 2024 (52,606 bytes), which prints the day it was captured
  and is otherwise fixed.
- **This packet's passes.** Every recorded response was downloaded at 2026-09-27T23:46:27Z-23:57:14Z and again at
  2026-09-28T00:29:03Z-00:37:00Z, 30 or more minutes apart for each source; all 71 matched the byte count and SHA-256 that the dossiers and
  checks recorded on 26 September 2026. The only failures were connection errors from web.archive.org on eight first
  requests, retried at 23:55-23:57 UTC. The live sites shura.gov.sa and laws.boe.gov.sa still timed out on 28 September.
- Each extract's `stability_check` gives its own download times, and `provenance_note` how its identity is formed.

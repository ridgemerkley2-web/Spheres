# S12 — Real squadrons from owned aircraft

Status: implemented; qualification in progress with S13–S15.

Air command forms named squadrons from delivered, certified aircraft revisions. The squadron holds an assignment against the national Arsenal, not another inventory. Resizing, changing revision and disbanding exchange existing claims; none creates aircraft, performs a refit or resets age. Aircraft committed to another squadron, transit, service or a refit cannot be reused. New purchases remain unassigned until explicitly assigned.

Legacy `air_gen*` holdings retain their exact fractional formation equivalents and their existing accounting. The screen identifies them separately and performs no rounding or conversion into physical aircraft. Creating the first squadron moves all custom aircraft in that country onto the explicit squadron mission path; unassigned custom aircraft then remain parked.

Acceptance evidence is retained with the [combined flight qualification](../S15/README.md): ownership and save validation, pending deliveries, refit/retirement exclusions, capacity checks, deterministic loss reconciliation and ordinary reviewed browser controls.

This session does not complete G3 or certify a full campaign.

# Materials: first-machinery AI integration

Local review, 2026-09-04. Ridge approved continuing AI integration after the
Materials pilot. This extends the existing daily Economic Competition policy;
it does not authorize publication, free starting factories, or household demand.

## The decision

The earlier AI required a processor or imports before choosing its first machine
shop. Materials contracts could only answer demand from already commissioned
consumers, so inherited capacity could not replace that first processor decision.

The AI now has a bounded **startup inventory intention**: 15 Materials packs,
one planning month for a basic machine shop. This is not public consumption,
a permanent stock tax, an automatic raw-import order, or achieved production.
Real warehouse construction needs are added before netting coverage when the
first machine is needed to finish a previously paid warehouse.

1. Count owned packs, incoming imports and still-deliverable domestic contracts
   once. Already paid processors, queued factories and fractional specialists
   retain their existing behavior; no full machinery line is forced on a module.
2. Consider a 30-day inherited Materials contract for the uncovered starter lot.
   The whole remaining raw-input lot must already be owned, after existing
   Materials reservations. A one-day quote is not multiplied into an unsupported
   month-long promise.
3. Preview the additional Materials power and proposed machinery together using
   the normal factory support planner. Generation and grids must exist now;
   unfinished utilities and efficiency upgrades are not treated as live.
4. Check the exact rounded Materials quote, current daily operating authority,
   both normal command prices and the AI's eight-point political reserve.
5. Commission the finite order and one machinery project through the ordinary
   command path. Signing grants no output. Preserve the resulting startup packs
   against automatic surplus sales while that consumer is being built.

If the physical plan is sound but funding or political capital is temporarily
short, retain the machinery budget target and wait without signing either new
commitment. This avoids repeatedly shifting funding between machinery and an
unnecessary processor. Missing physical supply retains the existing processor or
import fallback. Actual project work, operations, freight, funding and shortages
continue every day between the AI's 30-day reviews.

The same startup coverage can be replenished for a queued first machine after a
cancelled or expired order, but delivered stock and valid remaining commitments
are counted first. Actual ongoing machinery demand continues through the
existing Materials and goods-trade policy. Nothing instructs the player's nation
or changes its manual order/automatic-import boundary.

## Limits

- No new AI raw-procurement system is added in this slice. A backed domestic lot
  competes with existing import/processor options; raw stocks are never gifted.
- Readiness is a current preflight, not protection from a later war, competing
  resource use, changed budget or ownership loss. Operations still atomically
  stall when their complete bundle is missing.
- The startup lot is an explicit game policy, not a sourced 1990 consumption
  statistic. The inherited capacity estimate and GDP reconciliation are unchanged.
- Smaller module economies remain Materials specialists unless actual demand
  and existing eligibility support a larger investment. Universal factory
  growth or universal adoption is not an acceptance target.

## Daily scheduling

The extended pre-change baseline exposed a separate integration defect:
Economic Competition reviews run after industry has settled for the day, but
Materials used the signing date as its first work date. A 30-day AI contract
therefore had only 29 operating opportunities. All 44 baseline contracts expired;
fully supplied examples delivered 14.5 of 15 packs at a rate of 0.5 per day.

New orders signed after that day's production settlement begin service on the
next date and receive their full delivery window. Commands before settlement
can still begin today. The quote/refusal and order creation share that calendar
rule, future service cannot run early, and already-saved orders are not silently
extended. The census records the observed signing date separately from the
first actual output date.

## Verification approach

Focused fixtures separately test the pure intention, paired priced commands,
whole-lot raw checks, daily funding, combined political costs, import alternatives,
startup-stock retention, warehouse supply netting and save continuity. These are
explicit supplied/powered test fixtures, not fabricated historical starting data.

The natural-world census retains all 137 starting countries and now observes
first machinery orders, installation and actual output, paid processor output,
delivered imports, actual research consumption and bounded blocking reasons by
economic size. It neither forces adoption nor attributes a fungible consumed pack
to an untracked source. Prior pilot results remain in
[MATERIALS_OPERATIONS_RESULTS.md](MATERIALS_OPERATIONS_RESULTS.md); follow-up
measurements are recorded in
[MATERIALS_AI_INTEGRATION_RESULTS.md](MATERIALS_AI_INTEGRATION_RESULTS.md).
